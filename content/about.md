Tanuki is a Zola theme named after the playful Japanese raccoon dog. It has
four modes, chosen with `extra.mode` in `zola.toml`:

- **blog** / **site**: a personal site with a post list, tags, and an RSS feed (this demo)
- **docs**: documentation with a sidebar, versions, and an on-page table of contents
- **book**: an e-book with chapters and a print view
- **product**: a landing page for a tool or library

Source and full examples: [github.com/raskell-io/tanuki](https://github.com/raskell-io/tanuki).
